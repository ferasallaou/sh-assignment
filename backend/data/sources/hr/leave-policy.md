# Annual leave policy 2026

Every full-time employee gets **25 days** of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time staff get the same share of their working week.

## Carrying days over

- Up to **5 unused days** carry over into the next year.
- Carried-over days must be used by **31 March** of that year, or they are lost.
- Any unused days above 5 are lost on 31 December. They are not paid out.

## Booking leave

- Book leave in the HR system. Approved future leave counts as used straight away.
- Leave of more than 3 days in a row needs **two weeks' notice** and your manager's approval.
- Nobody may take more than 15 days in a row without HR approval.

## Your balance

Your personal balance (days left, taken and booked) is in the HR system. Ask your manager or HR if it looks wrong.
