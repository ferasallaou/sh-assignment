## Problem

Store staff use the retailer dashboard early in the morning and late at night. A bright screen in a dark stockroom is the most common usability complaint in our support tickets.

## Evidence

- 14 of 60 retailers in the October survey asked for it.
- It is the second most requested feature after bulk export.

## Proposal

Ship dark mode for the web dashboard first, then the mobile app.

## Open questions

- Do we need dark charts on day one, or can charts stay light at first?
